## [26.1.2.4]

* Added optional integrations that use Adamantite, Aeternium and Aurichalcum. Each one only loads when its mod is installed

## [26.1.2.3]

* FTB Armory pickaxes and AIOTs are in `minecraft:cluster_max_harvestables`, so amethyst and geore clusters drop full shards and Fortune applies (FTBTesting/Testing-Issues#4615)
* Adamantite, Aeternium and Aurichalcum rods can be crafted from two ingots, so the scythes are craftable again (FTBTesting/Testing-Issues#4563)
* Jade shows the Adamantite pickaxe as the harvest tool for Aurichalcum ore, a tier above netherite
